1. Behind on points? You’ve barely lost anything.

The maths most people miss: the entire group stage — all 72 matches across GS1, GS2 and GS3 — is worth just 216 points. The whole tournament is worth 659. So roughly two-thirds of every point is still on the table, all in the knockouts — where each correct call is worth more:

Round of 325 pts
Round of 167 pts
Quarter-finals10 pts
Semi-finals15 pts
Final25 pts

On top of that: a penalty-winner bonus on every knockout round — call the shootout right and you bank extra — and from the semi-finals onwards, nailing the exact scoreline scores more again.

A cold group stage is completely recoverable. One good knockout run and you’re right back in it. Nobody is out of this — even if the family dog ate your tipsheet along the way.
