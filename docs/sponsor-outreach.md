# Sponsor outreach — email templates

Short, warm emails to land a first direct sponsor. Replace `[bracketed]` bits.
Pair with [media-kit.md](media-kit.md). Keep it personal — these go to people who
already run a comp with us, so lead with that.

---

## A. Warm — to a business already running a comp (best converting)

**Subject:** A "presented by [Brand]" [Comp name] for the knockouts?

Hi [First name],

Thanks for running **[Comp name]** on TribePicks this World Cup — you're one of 200+ comps in the game.

As we head into the knockout rounds, we're opening a handful of sponsor spots and thought of you first. For **[Brand]** that could look like:

- **A branded comp** — your logo on [Comp name]'s leaderboard and invites ("presented by [Brand]"), and/or
- **A sponsored challenge** — you put up a small prize for a knockout match or round, and [Brand] is named on every fixture in it.

It puts you in front of an engaged, returning audience: **1,000+ active players, 50,000+ predictions, checking standings every matchday**, skewed to [AU / your market].

Worth a quick chat? I can send a one-pager or jump on a 10-min call — whatever's easier. Either way, thanks for being part of it.

Cheers,
[Your name] · TribePicks
[email] · tribepicks.com

## B. Cold — to a brand that fits the audience (beverage, sportswear, delivery, etc.)

**Subject:** Reaching 1,000+ active World Cup fans — sponsor spot

Hi [First name],

I run **TribePicks**, a World Cup 2026 prediction game with **1,000+ active players** making **50,000+ predictions** — football fans across [AU, the US and Europe] who come back every matchday.

We're opening a few sponsor placements for the knockout rounds (in-feed banner, a sponsored prize challenge, or a branded comp). Given **[Brand]**'s audience, it could be a clean, high-engagement fit — and we can run it as a flat fee, performance/affiliate, or in-kind prize.

One-pager attached. Open to a quick call this week?

Cheers,
[Your name] · TribePicks
[email] · tribepicks.com

---

### Tips
- Send **A** first — warm leads convert far better. Personalise the comp name.
- Attach/paste the media kit; don't over-explain in the email.
- Lead with the knockouts (urgency + the most-watched matches).
- One clear ask: a short call or a reply. Follow up once after ~4 days.
