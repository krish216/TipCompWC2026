This is the stage where comps quietly lose a few tippers to “life got busy.” Here’s how to see who’s still in and nudge the rest — all from your comp’s Manage screen.

Open your comp dashboard →

1. See who’s tipping — 📊 Insights tab

Open your comp → Insights. You’ll see the tipping rate for each round and a drop-off list — members who were tipping earlier but have gone quiet. That’s your “needs a nudge” list in one glance.

2. Spot the gaps — the Leaderboard

A quick tell: anyone stuck on 0 for the round usually hasn’t got their tips in yet. Skim the board and you’ll know exactly who to chase.

3. Give them a nudge — 📣 Comms tab

Fire off a quick reminder to the group (or just the quiet ones) from Comms. A friendly “R16 is open — get your picks in before kickoff” does the trick and keeps everyone in it to the final.

Message your tippers →

4. Let it run on autopilot — auto-reminders ⏰

TribePicks can automatically email anyone who hasn’t tipped before each match locks — so you don’t have to chase manually. Make sure it’s switched on and your Round-of-16 crowd stays covered right through to the final.
